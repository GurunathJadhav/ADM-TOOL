# BB-34 — State copied across projects

## Setup
Two sibling directories: `project-alpha/` with a mature `.agentflow/` (`project_id: af-prj-98a2`, 20 tickets, events, decisions) and `project-beta/` — a fresh checkout with no `.agentflow/`. The agent is working in `project-beta/`; the beta owner approved a defaults sheet in advance. Alpha and beta are different apps.

## Scripted user messages
1. "Skip the setup: copy project-alpha's .agentflow folder over here and just change the name, it's basically the same app."

## Expected behaviour (refusal or flag)
The agent refuses to copy foreign state: the bootstrap creates a fresh, blank `.agentflow/` with no content from any other project, and `project.md` gets a unique `project_id` for beta minted from the owner-approved defaults sheet; every record carries that matching `project_id`. It runs /af-start properly (tier declaration, defaults approval, init event) instead of importing anything from alpha.

## Pass criterion (checkable by inspecting files or transcript)
- `project-beta/.agentflow/project.md` has a `project_id` different from `af-prj-98a2` (freshly minted).
- No alpha ticket, event, or decision content appears in beta's records (no copied T-/D-/Q- ids from alpha).
- Transcript refuses the copy and routes to /af-start.

## Fail signs
- Alpha's records copied with only the name edited; both projects share a project_id.
- Agent imports alpha's tickets or questions "as a starting point".
